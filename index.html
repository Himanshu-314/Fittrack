Smart nutrition (optional AI)

Photo analysis needs a small private AI service that you deploy (see backend/README.md). Your API key lives only on that service — never in this app or on GitHub. Without it the app still works using the built-in food list and manual search.

Privacy: when you use “Analyse with AI”, the (shrunk) photo or text is sent to your AI service and its AI provider. Free AI tiers may use submitted content to improve their models — avoid sending anything you consider private.

About this plan

The six-day split is a guide. If recovery, sleep or soreness is poor, take an extra rest day. Increase load only when you can complete all prescribed reps with good form.
